put it in your own words, and post it. This file is public on purpose: treat it
as a starting point, not a script. Edit freely.

## Posting notes

- Keep your own voice. Short and dry tends to land better than hype.
- The install link is the main call to action; the plain play link is a lighter
  option for people who just want to look.
- Nothing here names a specific model on purpose. If you want to call out the one
  that annoyed you, add it yourself.
